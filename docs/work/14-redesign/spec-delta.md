# Spec delta — lens navigation, the Weekly horizon, Ideas removed

Amendment 2 to `docs/SPEC.md`. Read that document's §6 for the rule-by-rule before/after; this file is
what a builder needs to scope and start.

**In SPEC:** 56 rules added (155 → 211), 99 scenarios added (157 → 256), 84 existing rules marked `⚠`
superseded / retired / modified — **32 retired outright** — and 43 scenarios retired, superseded or
re-pointed, plus the whole `### Plan` and `### Idea` scenario sections.

**Amendment 1 was never built.** `docs/work/13-planning-ahead/` was halted mid-flight; the code is at
the pre-A1 state. So this is one change against the shipped code, not two. Two pieces of A1 survive and
should be re-planned into this build rather than rewritten: **future-period planning** (generalised here
from weeks to every horizon, and now uncapped) and **manual backlog ordering** (untouched — see §5).

---

## 1. The change, for a builder

Six product changes, one coherent redesign.

1. **Weekly becomes a fifth horizon.** `Life → Yearly → Quarterly → Monthly → Weekly`, ranks 0–4.
   Monthly stops being terminal and **Weekly becomes terminal in exactly the way Monthly was** — the
   existing "the shortest horizon can have no sub-goals" rule survives with one word replaced, and the
   single rank comparison in `checkCreate` keeps enforcing it. Levels may still be skipped, so a Weekly
   goal may hang off any longer horizon including Life (R-goal-30/31/32).
2. **`weekly_focus` is deleted.** A weekly intent is an ordinary goal with `horizon = 'Weekly'`, and
   several under one parent is how a week holds several intentions. Every concept derived from a focus
   row goes with it (R-rm-2).
3. **Only Weekly goals hold tasks.** The condition is `horizon = 'Weekly'`, **never leaf-ness** — a
   childless Monthly goal is a leaf and must never be a task parent. "Leaf" is retired as a product word
   rather than redefined, because the two notions stopped coinciding (R-goal-37, R-goal-39).
4. **Navigation is five lenses, not a tree.** A lens is a flat, account-wide list of one horizon, scoped
   to one period, grouped by owning Life goal. No filters — grouping replaces them. The Tasks screen,
   the plan screen and the goals tree all disappear into it (R-lens-1 … R-lens-16).
5. **Every non-Life goal gets a canonical `periodKey`.** Free-text `period` becomes a derived label. A
   lens must *partition* a horizon's goals, and free text partitions nothing (R-goal-33).
6. **Ideas are deleted outright** — no migration, no export (R-rm-1). And **the forward planning cap is
   gone**: any future period is writable, at every horizon (R-goal-36, R-lens-7).

Four rulings worth reading before you start.

- **A task's week stays its own stored field.** `origin_week_start` is seeded once from its Weekly
  goal's `periodKey` and is then immutable; it is never re-derived from the parent, and there is no
  week parameter on task create at all. Carry, uncheck and D-1 each independently require this: carry
  is a comparison against a stored date with no write and no job (the reason this product has no cron);
  uncheck needs the *original* origin to survive a completion and its reversal; and a week that is
  looked up rather than recorded changes meaning without a write, which is the worst thing this spec
  ever inherited. **What legitimately diverges is exactly one thing: the task carried** (R-task-40).
- **Carrying is what makes a Weekly goal reappear.** A Weekly goal shows in week `W` iff
  `periodKey = W` **or** it still holds an open task visible in `W`. The second kind renders in a
  separate **carried band** below the week's own goals, oldest first, labelled with its own week.
  Nothing ever ages out of it: the escalation is the red chip on the task, and there is only one
  (R-lens-12).
- **Carry age is now signed.** `weeksBetween(origin, min(viewed, current))`, unclamped: a task planned
  for `+1` is age `−1`. Nothing new renders — no label fires below 1 either way — but it leaves one
  guard instead of two, and it is a **silent wire break** (§3.2 #9).
- **Move-to-backlog had to move.** A task's owning goal is now a Weekly goal, and a Weekly goal may hold
  no backlog items. The item lands on the **nearest non-Weekly ancestor** (R-backlog-29). *This was a
  direct contradiction between R-task-15 and R-backlog-2 created by the redesign; it is the kind a
  review does not catch, because both rules read fine alone.*

## 2. Migration and compatibility

### 2.1 Database — one migration (`0002_*`), and it is destructive

`apps/api/migrations/` holds `0000_lovely_cammi.sql` and `0001_busy_mentor.sql` plus `meta/_journal.json`
and the snapshots, which **must be regenerated by drizzle-kit, never hand-edited**.

```sql
-- 1. Ideas: deleted outright. No conversion, no export (owner's decision).
DROP TABLE `ideas`;                       -- takes ix_ideas_owner with it

-- 2. weekly_focus: deleted outright. Rows are NOT converted into Weekly goals — see open question 2.
DROP TABLE `weekly_focus`;                -- takes ux_weekly_focus_goal_week and ix_weekly_focus_week

-- 3. Goals: the period key every lens filters on.
ALTER TABLE `goals` ADD COLUMN `period_key` TEXT NOT NULL DEFAULT '';
CREATE INDEX `ix_goals_lens` ON `goals` (`user_id`,`horizon`,`period_key`,`created_at`,`id`);
-- ix_goals_owner_parent stays: it still serves the sibling order and the ancestor walk.
```

- **`horizon` is a Drizzle `text({ enum: HORIZONS })`** — SQLite stores it as TEXT with no CHECK, so
  adding `'Weekly'` needs **no DDL**. Verify that against the generated snapshot before assuming it.
- **`period_key` needs a backfill, and it is a guess.** Parse the existing `period` label with the same
  grammar `defaultPeriod` emits (`2026`, `Q4 2026`, `Sep 2026`); anything unparseable or empty falls
  back to the period containing the goal's `created_at`, in the owner's timezone. **The migration must
  report how many rows it guessed at.** A Life goal's key is `''`. See open question 1.
- Existing goals are all Life/Yearly/Quarterly/Monthly, so no row becomes a Weekly goal and no task
  needs re-parenting: `tasks.goal_id` currently points at non-Life leaves, which under the new rule are
  **not** legal task parents. **This is the migration's one real data problem** — see open question 3.
- The migration is forward-only and drops two tables. There is no rollback that restores their rows.

### 2.3 The five silent breaks

Each compiles, type-checks, and is wrong at runtime.

1. **`CompleteTaskRequest.week` loses its guard** the moment `WeekOffset` widens (§2.2 #22). Inherited
   from A1's analysis; still the highest-value single line in the change.
2. **`TaskView.carryWeeks` goes signed** with no type error at any call site. Anything doing
   `if (carryWeeks)` or `carryWeeks >= 1` is fine; anything doing `carryWeeks > 0 ? … : …` as a proxy
   for "has a label" is fine; anything **summing** them, or feeding them to a `nonnegative` parse on the
   client, is not.
3. **`goal-tree.ts`'s whole-list scans** are premised on "at most 500 nodes, 4 levels deep — so nothing
   here needs a query", stated in its own header. With Weekly goals an account grows ~1,000 goals a
   year (SPEC Q-12, rewritten). `isLeaf` is a `.some` over every goal and is called from inside
   `orderedTree`'s walk: O(n²). Nothing throws; the app just gets slower every week. **This is the
   highest-risk line in the change** and the reason R-lens-16 exists.
4. **`rank()` throws on an unknown horizon.** Any persisted or cached payload carrying `'Weekly'` that
   reaches a build with the 4-member array raises `RangeError`, and there are **three copies of
   `HORIZONS`** (shared, `domain/enums`, `domain/goal-tree`) plus an inline literal in
   `mcp/shapes.ts:248`. Ship all four in one change or the MCP surface disagrees with the API.
5. **`period` becomes server-owned.** A client that keeps sending its old free-text `period` gets no
   error — the value is simply ignored — and the goal quietly lands in whatever period `periodKey` says.
   The only guard is that `periodKey` is required.

## 4. Open questions for the owner

1. **How should unparseable `period` labels be backfilled?** `[recommended]` Parse what matches the
   grammar the app itself emits; fall back to the period containing the goal's `createdAt`. It is a
   guess, but it puts the goal in a lens where its owner will find it, which beats leaving it
   unreachable. The migration reports the count it guessed at.
2. **Are past weeks allowed to render empty after the migration?** `weekly_focus` rows are dropped, not
   converted, so every past week loses its focus sentences and shows only its tasks. `[recommended]`
   Accept it. Converting each row into a Weekly goal would manufacture history — goals claiming to have
   existed in past weeks, which R-lens-10 forbids on principle. **This is the one decision in the
   redesign that cannot be undone**, so it should be said out loud before the migration runs.
3. **What happens to existing tasks, whose `goal_id` points at a non-Life leaf that is not a Weekly
   goal?** Every current task is illegal under R-goal-39 the moment the rule lands. `[recommended]` For
   each distinct `(goal_id, origin_week_start)` among open tasks, create one Weekly goal under that goal
   titled from the focus sentence for that week if one exists and from the goal's own title otherwise,
   and re-point the tasks at it. This is the only place in the migration where a `weekly_focus` sentence
   is read, and it is read to keep *work* legal rather than to reconstruct a plan — which is why it does
   not contradict question 2. Done and exited tasks may be re-pointed the same way or left pointing at a
   non-Weekly goal as inert history; **prefer re-pointing**, so no query has to special-case them.
4. **May a Weekly goal be re-parented?** SPEC §4 Q-24. This spec keeps Move available and reads the
   owner's "never re-parented or moved forward" as one statement about the **week**. `[recommended]`
   Keep Move: forbidding it makes Weekly the only horizon that cannot be corrected. If the owner meant
   Move too, R-goal-40 loses one clause and nothing else changes.
5. **Is `Repeat last week` per Life line or account-wide?** `[recommended]` Per line, from the group
   header. Account-wide creates twenty goals in one tap with no review.
6. **Should the carried band be collapsible?** `[recommended]` Yes, as a whole, remembered per session,
   never per goal — so a week with twelve carried goals still opens on this week's plan. It is
   presentation and adds no state to any row.
7. **Do the new caps hold?** SPEC Q-12 raises children-per-goal 100 → 1,000 and goals-per-owner
   500 → 10,000. `[recommended]` Yes, but treat the **query strategy** as the real deliverable: the
   caps are only safe once `goal-tree.ts`'s whole-list O(n²) scans are replaced by period-scoped reads
   (R-lens-16). Raising the numbers alone ships a product that degrades weekly.
8. **Does the Weekly lens need its own tab after all?** `[recommended]` No. It is where the owner will
   spend most of their time, which argues for it, but a five-way switcher plus a Weekly tab means two
   ways to reach one screen and a tab bar that disagrees with the switcher about where you are.
9. **Should `+ New goal` on a lens create at that lens's horizon?** `[recommended]` Yes — creating a
   Monthly goal from the Monthly lens, pre-filled with the selected period, is the obvious reading, and
   it is the only way the period control ever pre-fills a create.
10. **Is `planned N weeks ago` still wanted on a Weekly goal?** `[recommended]` Yes, unchanged from A1's
    ruling: one muted line, on arrival only, at a ≥2-week threshold. Anything more is the review wizard
    the product removed on purpose.
